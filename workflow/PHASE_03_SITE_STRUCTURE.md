# Phase 03: Strategy And Positioning

Status: Draft

## Purpose

Define positioning, offer clarity, value proposition, trust elements, claim safety, content direction, and SEO direction before site structure and content production.

## When To Use This Phase

Use this phase after PROJECT_BRIEF.md is usable and before finalizing routes, page clusters, or design direction.

## Main Questions

- What is the project's core positioning?
- What offer must be clear on the site?
- What audience objections must be answered?
- What trust elements are real and usable?
- Which claims are approved?
- What content direction supports conversion?
- What SEO direction should guide page structure?
- Which assumptions need Rosen / MindGrid Studio approval?

## Required Inputs

- PROJECT_BRIEF.md.
- PROJECT_RULES.md.
- Known competitors/reference sites.
- Known services/products/solutions.
- Approved claims and proof points.

## Expected Outputs

- Positioning notes.
- Approved value proposition direction.
- Claim safety notes.
- SEO direction notes.
- DECISIONS_LOG.md entries where real decisions are made.
- NEXT_ACTIONS.md updated with structure/content tasks.

## Recommended Steps

1. Review intake docs.
2. Clarify offer and audience.
3. Identify objections and proof points.
4. Separate approved claims from assumptions.
5. Draft positioning direction.
6. Identify SEO/page cluster implications.
7. Log real decisions.
8. Prepare site structure task.

## Codex / Agent Involvement

- ChatGPT Lead Assistant leads strategy.
- Content Strategist and SEO/AEO Specialist may support.
- Codex should not implement unless asked to update docs within clear scope.

## Tracking Docs Update Rules

- Update CURRENT_STATUS.md after strategy direction is usable.
- Update NEXT_ACTIONS.md with site structure/content tasks.
- Update ISSUES_LOG.md for unresolved claims or missing proof.
- Update DECISIONS_LOG.md for real strategic/content/SEO decisions.

## Completion Criteria

- Positioning is clear enough to guide routes and copy.
- Claim risks are identified.
- SEO direction is documented.
- Assumptions requiring approval are listed.

## Stop Conditions

- Offer is unclear.
- Claims cannot be verified.
- Audience objections are unknown.
- SEO direction conflicts with business goals.
- Required approval is missing.

## Handoff Notes

New sessions should inspect PROJECT_BRIEF.md, PROJECT_RULES.md, DECISIONS_LOG.md, SEO_PLAN.md if started, and CURRENT_STATUS.md.
